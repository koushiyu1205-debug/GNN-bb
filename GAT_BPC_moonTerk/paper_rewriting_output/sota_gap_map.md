## Gap Summary

- **Gap 1 — proof-preserving pricing and branching integration:** Test whether learned pricing prioritization and secondary branch-candidate ranking can improve the fixed logical-path solution space lunar multi-sortie/fleet BPC while cut control remains deterministic and formal proof, exactness guarantees, and proofs remain exclusively on the exact path.
- **Gap 2 — empirical validation:** Supply preregistered no-guidance controls, ablations, fallback/equivalence audits, and held-out scale tests; until those learning-performance experiments exist, current diagnostics support only a validation plan and no performance claim.
