## Selection Rules for Drafting

1. Select a source only after mapping it to a concrete manuscript claim.
2. Prefer publisher, proceedings, or local full-text evidence over search
   snippets.
3. Do not use `[VERIFY]` entries for central novelty, exactness, or performance
   claims until their metadata and supporting passages are checked.
4. Treat C001 and C002 as mandatory prior-art checks for any learning-guided
   branching or pricing claim.
5. Cite project-generated results only through frozen project artifacts; the
   external literature cannot validate this project's speed, closure rate, or
   proofs.
