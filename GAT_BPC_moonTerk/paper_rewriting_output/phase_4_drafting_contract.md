## Completion Meaning

Phase 4 is complete when the working manuscript has a continuous title,
abstract, Introduction, Related Work, formulation, method, experimental
design, results, discussion, conclusion, appendices, and draft reference-key
map. A section may contain an explicit `TBD` block only where the required
model, training artifact, run, or figure does not exist.

An empty empirical slot does not remove its research question, baseline,
metric, safety gate, or later interpretive location. The argument therefore
remains:

1. define the lunar fleet-routing decision and exactness scope;
2. formulate multi-trip routes and the normalized objective;
3. separate learned ordering from proof-producing exact logic;
4. pre-register exact-safety and workload comparisons;
5. report available exact-framework evidence;
6. leave learning-effect and seasonal operating-phase results empty until
   their frozen evidence exists;
7. state conclusions at the maturity supported today.

## Placeholder Syntax

Missing content uses exactly one of:

- `[[TBD-M001: ...]]` through `[[TBD-M006: ...]]` for missing artifacts;
- `[[TBD-EXP-L0: ...]]`, `[[TBD-EXP-L1: ...]]`,
  `[[TBD-EXP-L2: ...]]`, `[[TBD-EXP-G: ...]]`, or
  `[[TBD-EXP-EPOCH: ...]]` for experiment blocks;
- `[[TBD-FIG15: ...]]` or `[[TBD-FIG16: ...]]` for evidence-dependent figures;
- `[[TBD-ABS-RESULT: ...]]` for the final abstract result sentence.

No placeholder contains a guessed number, expected direction, provisional
conclusion, or favorable adjective.
