# TRC Exemplar Inventory

These six papers are the `pro`-tier target-scene set. They are used to learn
structure and rhetoric, not to import their claims into the manuscript.

| ID | Paper | Year | DOI | Structural Reason |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| TGT001 | Bezzi, Ceselli, and Righini, “A route-based algorithm for the electric vehicle routing problem with multiple technologies” | 2023 | `10.1016/j.trc.2023.104374` | Defines a technology-driven routing problem, introduces an extended route formulation, develops exact pricing, then proves computational value |
| TGT002 | Cabrera, Cordeau, and Mendoza, “Solving the park-and-loop routing problem by branch-price-and-cut” | 2023 | `10.1016/j.trc.2023.104369` | Closest structural analogue: nonstandard route composition, set-covering master, tailored pricing, subset-row inequalities and explicit optimality proofs |
| TGT003 | Sakarya et al., “Two-echelon prize-collecting vehicle routing with time windows and vehicle synchronization” | 2025 | `10.1016/j.trc.2024.104987` | Connects a complex exact routing method to system design and managerial implications |
| TGT004 | Xu et al., “An exact algorithm for unpaired pickup and delivery vehicle routing problem with multiple commodities and multiple visits” | 2024 | `10.1016/j.trc.2024.104488` | Moves from an operational pain point to model, valid inequalities, exact algorithm, real-case comparison and sensitivity analysis |
| TGT005 | Jeong et al., “Adaptive robust electric vehicle routing under energy consumption uncertainty” | 2024 | `10.1016/j.trc.2024.104529` | Demonstrates how energy constraints are made operationally meaningful through robustness and trade-off analysis |
| TGT006 | Qin and Pournaras, “Coordination of drones at scale” | 2023 | `10.1016/j.trc.2023.104387` | Shows TRC’s technology-to-transportation-system bridge for autonomous sensing fleets and open-data evaluation |
