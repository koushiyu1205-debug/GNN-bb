# TRC Official Scope and Access Note

Checked: 2026-07-23.

## Verified from the Official Journal Landing Page

- *Transportation Research Part C: Emerging Technologies* seeks work on the
  development, application and implications of emerging technologies in
  transportation systems.
- Its stated intellectual core is transportation-system impact rather than an
  isolated technology.
- Quantitative methods from operations research, control, networks, computer
  science and AI are explicitly welcomed.
- The scope highlights efficiency, safety, reliability, resource consumption,
  logistics, autonomous vehicles, aviation and infrastructure/resource
  management.
- The journal page gives special emphasis to open science and datasets that
  support transferability and benchmarking.

Official source:
<https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/transportation-research-part-c-emerging-technologies>

## Fit Constraint for This Project

The manuscript cannot rely on “lunar technology” alone. It needs to present a
transportation-system problem: resource-constrained autonomous fleet routing
for a remote exploration/logistics network, with measurable implications for
operational efficiency, safety, reliability and resource use. A purely
aerospace-navigation or algorithm-engineering story has material desk-rejection
risk and may fit *Acta Astronautica* or a methodological OR journal better.
